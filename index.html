Phase 10 Score Keeper

Phase 10 score card for game night.

Add your players, hit start, and keep the game moving — this app tracks everyone's phase, running score, and whose turn it is to deal, right from a phone on the table.

  • Tracks each player's current phase automatically
  • Rotates the dealer for you every round
  • Works in any phone or tablet browser — no app to install
  • Nothing leaves your device — no account, no ads, no tracking

Unofficial and fan-made — not affiliated with Phase 10 Games or Ken Johnson, who holds the Phase 10 copyright. There isn't an official score app, so fans built this one for game night. To play the real thing, get the game at phase10.com.

    Privacy & cookies

    Last updated: 1 September 2026

    This notice is for phase10.live, an unofficial fan-made Phase 10 score keeper. It explains what this site does — and does not — do with information on your device.

    Cookies

    This site does not set cookies. There is no cookie banner because we do not use advertising, analytics, or optional tracking cookies.

    Your browser may still store a small amount of data locally so the score keeper can work. That storage is described below. It is used only to provide the tool you asked for, so we do not ask for cookie consent.

    What stays on your device

    The app runs entirely in your browser. To remember a game if you refresh the page or come back later, it saves the current session in your browser's local storage (not a cookie), under the name phase10.session.v1. That can include:

    • Player names you type in
    • Scores, current phase, dealer, round number, and round history

    This information is not uploaded to us. We do not have user accounts, and we cannot see your scores or names from our side.

    What we do not do

    • No advertising or ad networks
    • No analytics, tags, or tracking pixels
    • No selling or sharing of game data
    • No sign-up or mailing list

    Hosting

    The site is a static website. The company that hosts it may process standard technical logs (for example IP address, browser type, and the page requested) in order to deliver the files and keep the service secure. We do not use those logs for marketing. GitHub Pages currently hosts this site; GitHub's own privacy statement covers that hosting.

    External links

    Links to other websites, including phase10.com, have their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for those sites.

    How to delete the saved game

    Use New Game in the app to clear the current session. You can also delete site data for phase10.live in your browser settings, which removes the local storage entry.

    Your rights

    Because game details stay on your device, you control them there. If names you enter are personal data, they are processed on your device so the score keeper can function. We do not hold a copy on a server to access, correct, or export for you.

    If you have a question about this notice, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
