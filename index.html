Phase 10 Score Keeper

The free, no-signup Phase 10 score card for game night.

Add your players, hit start, and keep the game moving — this app tracks everyone's phase, running score, and whose turn it is to deal, right from a phone on the table.

  • Tracks each player's current phase automatically
  • Rotates the dealer for you every round
  • Works in any phone or tablet browser — no app to install
  • Nothing leaves your device — no account, no ads, no tracking

How it works

  1. Add your players. Type each name and hit add.
  2. Set the seating order and dealer. Drag to reorder, then pick who deals first.
  3. Score each round. Enter points and check off anyone who completed their phase — totals and the dealer update automatically.

Frequently asked questions

How does scoring work in Phase 10?

Each round, players who don't complete their phase add up the point value of the cards left in their hand. This app doesn't tally card values for you — enter whatever your table agrees the round's points are, and check "Completed phase" for anyone who finished and can move on to the next phase.

Who deals first in Phase 10?

Deal traditionally passes to the left each round. Pick a starting dealer during setup and this app rotates it automatically after every round is submitted.

Is this app free to use?

Yes — completely free, with no account, sign-up, or ads.

Does it save my game if I close the browser?

Yes. Your current game is saved to your browser's local storage, so reopening the page picks up right where you left off. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — scores stay on your device.
